\paragraph{Input Preprocessing} Every volume is reoriented to the frame of the backbone's pretraining and resampled to its grid. TAP-CT: LPS, native slice spacing, in-plane trilinear resize to $224\times224$, depth padded with air to at least 12 slices and to a multiple of 4; HU clipped to $[-1008, 822]$ and standardized with the model's mean and standard deviation; windows of 12 slices with a stride of 3 slices plus a final window ending at the last slice (at most 32 windows per training step, all windows at evaluation). SPECTRE: RAS, $0.5\times0.5\times1$\,mm, centered $512\times512\times256$ field of view padded with the scan minimum, HU in $[-1000, 1000]$ scaled to $[0,1]$; a $4\times4\times4$ grid of 64 crops of $128\times128\times64$ voxels at evaluation and a random contiguous $2\times2\times2$ sub-grid of 8 crops at training; each crop is summarized by its CLS token concatenated with its mean patch token before the feature combiner. Pillar-0: LPI, $0.5\times0.5\times1.25$\,mm, centered $128\times256\times256$ volume, eleven input channels (ten fixed center/width windows and a per-volume min--max channel), one forward pass; the linear head reads the model's L2-normalized pooled embedding (three scales concatenated, 1152-d), the attentive head reads the tokens of the three scales, any scale with more than 4096 tokens being average-pooled on its grid. For segmentation, TAP-CT keeps the classification grid (in-plane 224, native depth) and crops of $12\times224\times224$ voxels; SPECTRE resamples to 1\,mm and uses crops of $64\times128\times128$ voxels. A training crop is centered on a random foreground voxel of the run's structures with probability 0.67 and placed uniformly otherwise; volumes smaller than the crop are padded with the image minimum. At evaluation the whole volume is covered by a sliding window with Gaussian weighting (overlap 0.5 for TAP-CT, 0.25 for SPECTRE) and Dice is computed on the backbone's working grid, without post-processing.

\paragraph{Heads} The linear head is dropout (0.2) followed by one linear layer on the pooled feature: the mean of the window CLS tokens for TAP-CT (768-d), the feature combiner's CLS token for SPECTRE (1080-d) and the pooled image embedding for Pillar-0 (1152-d). The attentive probe prepends a learnable CLS token to the token set (the window CLS tokens for TAP-CT, the combiner tokens for SPECTRE and the multi-scale tokens for Pillar-0), applies two pre-norm transformer blocks with eight-head self-attention and a GELU feed-forward layer of ratio 4, normalizes the CLS output and maps it through dropout (0.2) and a linear layer. For one label, the linear head has 769, 1081 and 1153 parameters and the attentive probe 14.2, 28.0 and 31.9\,M on TAP-CT, SPECTRE and Pillar-0, respectively. LoRA+ABMIL pools the window or crop tokens by gated attention \citep{ilse2018abmil}, $a_i=\mathrm{softmax}_i\big(\bm{w}^\top(\tanh(\bm{V}\bm{x}_i)\odot\sigma(\bm{U}\bm{x}_i))\big)$ with hidden size 128, and feeds the pooled token to the attentive probe. The segmentation linear probe is a $1\times1\times1$ convolution on the deepest grid (TAP-CT block 12, SPECTRE block 24), upsampled trilinearly. The decoder projects each of the four grids to 128 channels with a $1\times1\times1$ convolution, resizes them to a common grid (the token grid), concatenates them (512 channels), fuses them with a $3^3$ convolution, instance normalization and leaky ReLU to 128 channels, then applies stages of trilinear $\times2$ upsampling followed by the same convolution block that halves the channels (to a minimum of 16) until the voxel grid is reached (three stages for TAP-CT, four for SPECTRE), and ends with a $1\times1\times1$ convolution to one logit per structure. It has 2.45\,M (TAP-CT) and 2.62\,M (SPECTRE) parameters plus $17C$ or $33C$ for $C$ structures, and no skip connections from the input.

\paragraph{Adapters} LoRA \citep{hu2022lora} replaces a frozen projection $\bm{W}_0\bm{x}$ by $\bm{W}_0\bm{x}+\tfrac{\alpha}{r}\bm{B}\bm{A}\,\mathrm{drop}(\bm{x})$ with $r=16$, $\alpha=32$ and dropout 0.1; $\bm{A}$ is initialized Kaiming-uniform and $\bm{B}$ at zero, so training starts from the pretrained model. It is applied to the fused query/key/value projection of every TAP-CT block and to the separate query and key/value projections of every SPECTRE block, of SPECTRE's feature combiner, and of every Pillar-0 block. Late adapters cover TAP-CT blocks 9--12, SPECTRE blocks 17--24 and its feature combiner and the third stage of Pillar-0 (8 of 12 blocks). rsLoRA scales the update by $\alpha/\sqrt{r}$ instead of $\alpha/r$. DoRA \citep{liu2024dora} rescales each row of the adapted weight to a learned magnitude $\bm{m}$, $\bm{W}=\bm{m}\odot(\bm{W}_0+\tfrac{\alpha}{r}\bm{B}\bm{A})/\lVert\bm{W}_0+\tfrac{\alpha}{r}\bm{B}\bm{A}\rVert_{\text{row}}$, with $\bm{m}$ initialized to the row norms of $\bm{W}_0$; the magnitude is a two-dimensional parameter and receives weight decay like the adapter matrices. Attention-only fine-tuning unfreezes every parameter of the attention modules that LoRA would adapt (query, key, value and output projections with their biases). On Pillar-0, the per-scale blocks of scales that have already left the encoder and the text projection are also wrapped or unfrozen, but they never run and receive no gradient; counting them, LoRA updates 1.1\,M, LoRA$_{\text{late}}$ 0.74\,M, Attn-only FT 21\,M and Full FT 94\,M parameters (Figure~\ref{fig:arms}c excludes them). In segmentation the SPECTRE adapters and full fine-tuning also cover the feature combiner, which the segmentation path does not evaluate; those parameters receive no gradient.

\paragraph{Two-stage Methods} Lin$\to$LoRA and Attn$\to$LoRA take the probe weights from the parent run's checkpoint at step 4000 of its 8000-step schedule, add late-block LoRA with $\bm{B}=0$, and train 4000 further steps with a fresh optimizer and schedule. Dec$\to$LoRA takes the decoder from the Dec run at the midpoint of its schedule (step 4000; step 12\,000 for the 24\,000-step joint models) and trains all-block LoRA with the decoder for the second half.

\paragraph{Optimization} We used AdamW with the default $\beta=(0.9, 0.999)$ and $\epsilon=10^{-8}$ in all runs. The learning rate rises linearly from 1\% of its peak over the first 10\% of the steps and then follows a cosine to $10^{-6}$. Weight decay is applied to parameters of dimension two or more, excluding biases and normalization weights. Gradients are not clipped. Classification trains at batch size 1, chosen empirically, without gradient accumulation; segmentation at a micro-batch of 2 crops with accumulation 2, so four crops per step. The classification loss for a case with logits $\bm{z}$, labels $\bm{y}$ and known-label mask $\bm{m}$ is
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{L}_{\text{cls}}=\frac{1}{B}\sum_{b=1}^{B}\sum_{t} m_{bt}\,\mathrm{BCE}(z_{bt}, y_{bt}; w_t), \qquad w_t=\mathrm{clip}\Big(\frac{n^{-}_t}{n^{+}_t}, 0.05, 20\Big),
\label{eq:lcls}
\end{equation}
where $\mathrm{BCE}(\cdot;w_t)$ is the binary cross-entropy with positive weight $w_t$ computed from the negative and positive counts of label $t$ in the training split. The segmentation loss per case $b$ and structure $c$, with $\bm{p}=\sigma(\bm{z})$ over the $V$ voxels of a crop and reference $\bm{y}$, is
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{L}_{\text{seg}}=\frac{1}{BC}\sum_{b,c}\Big[1-\frac{2\sum_v p_{bcv}y_{bcv}+1}{\sum_v p_{bcv}+\sum_v y_{bcv}+1}+\frac{1}{V}\sum_v \mathrm{BCE}(z_{bcv},y_{bcv})\Big].
\label{eq:lseg}
\end{equation}
In the joint CVPR26 models the three sources are sampled in the ratio 10:8:8 (AMOS, COVID-CTset, LUNA25); every other run cycles one shuffled training set.

\paragraph{Model Selection and Per-case Outputs} Every 1000 steps the selection and held-out splits are evaluated and a checkpoint of the trained parameters is written; the checkpoint with the highest selection macro AUROC (mean over labels with both classes present) or mean Dice (mean over structures of the mean over cases in which the structure is present in the reference or the prediction) is kept, ties keeping the earlier checkpoint. For TotalSegmentator only the selection split is evaluated during training; the held-out split is evaluated afterwards with the selected checkpoint, and the recomputed selection Dice is required to match the recorded value within 0.005. Per case, the held-out probability of every known label (classification) and the Dice of every structure (segmentation) at the selected checkpoint are stored and are the input of every number in this paper. Dice thresholds the logits at 0; a structure enters a case's mean when the reference or the prediction contains it, so a false-positive structure scores 0 and an absent, unpredicted structure is skipped.
